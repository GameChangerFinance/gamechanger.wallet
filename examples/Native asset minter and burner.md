
# Cardano Example Dapp

## **Native asset minter and burner**

Creates a native script and uses it as policy to mint two fungible tokens under it, then burns one of them in a second transaction while the other survives. Asset names and quantities are checked with assert() before the wallet builds anything, remove them to explore different outcomes and balance states.
