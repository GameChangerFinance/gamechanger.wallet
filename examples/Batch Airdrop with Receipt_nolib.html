Cardano Dapp: "Batch Airdrop with Receipt"

Airdrops an existing native asset to five recipients in a single transaction and stamps a CIP-20 receipt on-chain: a SHA-256 digest of the canonical recipient manifest. Anyone can later recompute the digest from the published list and prove the drop was not altered, without trusting the sender. Minimum ADA per output is auto-computed by buildTx, and the change optimizer is left enabled to keep the UTXO set healthy.
